How It Works

Three simple steps — no technical expertise required.

📝

1. Register once

Donors sign up with their blood group, phone number, and location. It takes under two minutes.

🔍

2. Search in real time

Patients, hospitals, or NGOs search by blood group and city to find nearby, currently-available donors.

📞

3. Connect directly

Contact details are revealed with consent, so requesters can call the donor directly — no middleman, no delay.

Why This Platform

Built to close the gaps in existing, fragmented donor directories.

⚡

Real-time availability

Donors toggle their status active/inactive after donating, so search results are always current.

📍

Proximity-aware

Uses live location to rank donors by distance, not just by city name.

🌐

Built for smaller cities

Lightweight and simple — designed for towns underserved by metro-focused apps.
